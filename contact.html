Frequently asked questions

Where does the data come from?

Every variant is pulled from myvariant.info, which aggregates ClinVar, gnomAD, dbNSFP, and dbSNP into one source.

How often is the database updated?

An automated job refreshes the dataset weekly, pulling the latest ClinVar classifications directly into Locus.

Is Locus meant for clinical decision-making?

Locus is a research and educational tool. Always verify findings against primary sources and consult a qualified professional before making clinical decisions.

Can I report incorrect or outdated data?

Yes -- use the message form below or email us directly. We look into every report.
