Our mission

Every clinician and researcher deserves fast, transparent access to variant evidence -- not buried across disconnected databases. Locus brings together ClinVar's clinical classifications, gnomAD's population frequency data, and computational evidence scores into one clear, searchable view, so decisions are grounded in the fullest picture available.
