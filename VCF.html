VCF Risk Scan

Upload a sequenced VCF file. We check every variant in it against our ClinVar-backed database and surface only the ones flagged Pathogenic or Likely pathogenic -- the tier Franklin treats as clinically significant.

Read this first: this checks your file against a sample of ~15,000-20,000 ClinVar variants, not the full ClinVar database. A "no matches" result means nothing dangerous was found in that sample -- it is not a clean bill of health. This tool assumes a GRCh38-aligned VCF and is for exploration/demo purposes only, not a diagnosis. Your file is parsed entirely in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.
